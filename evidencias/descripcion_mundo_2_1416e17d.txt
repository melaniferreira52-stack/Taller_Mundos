At dawn a forest of crystal trees rises like a lattice of prisms, each trunk reflecting a spectrum of green, lime, blue, pink, and white light that ripple across the mist. The trees hum a crystalline choir, their leaves shimmering with soft turquoise and pale pink glints, while shafts of pale blue dawn light filter through, casting a silvery glow on the mossy floor. A narrow silver stream winds between the trunks, reflecting the pastel colors in its rippling surface. The composition balances the vertical lines of the glassy trunks against a hazy, pastel‑colored sky, rendered in a hyper‑realistic yet dreamlike, ethereal style.
