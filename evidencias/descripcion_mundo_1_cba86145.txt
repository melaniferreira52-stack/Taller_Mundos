In the foreground, a jagged archipelago of glassy floating islands hovers above a swirling violet cloudscape, each island’s spires silhouetted against a bruised blue horizon. A jagged streak of electric red lightning slices through the sky, illuminating patches of verdant gardens and golden solar towers in stark, dramatic contrast. Suspended cables and luminous walkways crisscross the islands, creating a lattice of reflective lines that lead the eye into the storm’s eye where a muted, diffuse glow diffuses the violet and green hues. The composition balances the upward thrust of the floating archipelago with the downward weight of the tempest, rendered in a hyper‑realist cyber‑fantasy style that emphasizes sharp edges and high‑contrast lighting.
